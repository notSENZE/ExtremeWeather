# ABOUT EXTREME WEATHER
**Extreme Weather** makes raids more atmospheric by introducing severe weather conditions such as thunderstorms, lightning, powerful winds, dense fog, and heavy rain or snowfall.

**SPT version:** `~4.1.6`  
**Dependencies:** None  
**FIKA compatibility:** Unknown  
**Recommended mod:** [Bodycam](https://github.com/notSENZE/Bodycam)  
**Known incompatibilities:** [Time & Weather Changer NG](https://sp-mod.com/mod/2120/time-weather-changer-ng), [Return Of Thunderstorms](https://sp-mod.com/mod/3236/return-of-thunderstorms)


# DETAILS
Whenever a raid begins with rain or snowfall, there is a configurable chance that the weather will gradually develop into a severe storm. You can adjust this probability through the `F12` BepInEx configuration menu.  
**Extreme Weather** integrates with Tarkov's existing weather system and allows storms to build naturally over time. A raid may begin with light rain before gradually progressing into heavier precipitation, dense fog, powerful winds, thunder, and lightning.  
Press the `Up Arrow` key during a raid to open the mod's control panel. From there, you can adjust the current time and weather conditions or trigger an extreme storm manually.  
Lightning can occasionally strike near the player. A nearby strike may temporarily impair your vision and cause tinnitus.  
At maximum storm intensity, bot visibility is limited to 70 metres by default. This distance can be adjusted through the `F12` configuration menu. Please note that this does not affect bot hearing or erase information about targets they have already detected.

**Bodycam Compatibility**
If you also have my [Bodycam](https://github.com/notSENZE/Bodycam) mod installed, powerful lightning events and nearby strikes can cause electrical interference.
Bodycam version **1.1.0 or later** is required for this feature.
